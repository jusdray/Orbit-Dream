# Visual Direction Notes

## Candidate approaches

### Theme Name: Orbital Editorial
**Very Brief Intro:** A cinematic, information-first space interface where the horizon frames the message instead of competing with it. Spacious type, restrained light, and a grounded Earth create calm technical authority.

**Probability:** 0.083

## Chosen approach: Orbital Editorial

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial sci-fi with the visual discipline of satellite imagery and premium technology annual reports.

**Core Principles:** The hero copy is always the primary focal point; visual motion supports reading rather than competes with it; the Earth is a horizon-scale spatial cue rather than a centre-stage object; interactions use restrained cyan illumination to confirm intention.

**Color Philosophy:** Deep blue-black and sea-green establish a quiet, expansive field. Cyan is reserved for active technology, key calls to action, and short-lived focus moments so its glow signals meaningful interaction.

**Layout Paradigm:** A vertically sequenced cinematic narrative. Major scenes are placed on an editorial axis, with the text plane held clear above a lower atmospheric horizon instead of competing for the centre of the frame.

**Signature Elements:** A lowered Earth horizon; soft cyan atmospheric bloom; asymmetric alternating service cards that move across a clear, dark field.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Controls visibly acknowledge intent with quick, contained illumination and predictable navigation. Smooth scrolling carries users to relevant information without a disruptive jump.

**Animation:** Hero motion remains secondary to reading; atmospheric visual motion is slow and low contrast. Service frames use clear 100% opacity holds before fading. Interface hover responses stay within 160–300ms and respect reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** Nohemi is the display and interface family, using heavy sentence-case headlines and compact uppercase labels. Supporting text has generous line height and reduced opacity to establish hierarchy under the hero message.

**Brand Essence:** SoftSeer makes enterprise technology feel intelligent, connected, and human-scale for organisations navigating what comes next. **Precise, expansive, assured.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are forward-looking and declarative; CTAs are direct, conversational, and specific. Example lines: “Looking Into the Future Together.” and “Reach out to map the next move.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** Retain the supplied SoftSeer logotype and use its compact cyan visual language as the primary recognisable mark.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Signal Cyan — #04D9FF.**

## Style Decisions

- Signal Cyan is the only high-energy accent; warm headline emphasis is excluded from all primary messaging and CTAs.
- Large dark intervals must frame a transition or focal point, rather than read as unintentional empty space.
- The compact SoftSeer mark and wordmark establish the first-screen identity and recur as subtle interface motifs.
- Planetary imagery remains a low-scale atmospheric orientation device, not a repeated centre-stage object.
- Editorial scroll scenes are compact enough that visual pauses read as transitions between statements, never as unmarked empty fields.
